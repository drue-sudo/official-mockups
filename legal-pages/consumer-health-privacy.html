Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1st, 2026

Table of Contents

  2. Definitions
  3. Categories of Consumer Health Data We Collect

  5. Contact Us
01

Introduction

Core DT Solutions LLC (“DiagnoseThis,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is committed to protecting your Consumer Health Data. This Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy (“Policy”) describes how we collect, use, share, and protect Consumer Health Data obtained through our website, mobile application, and related services (collectively, the “Services”).

This Policy supplements our Privacy Policy and applies specifically to Consumer Health Data as defined by applicable state laws, including the Washington My Health My Data Act (MHMD), Nevada SB370, and similar state regulations. By using our Services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy.

02

Definitions

The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

“Consumer Health Data” means personal information that is linked or reasonably linkable to a consumer and that identifies the consumer’s past, present, or future physical or mental health status. This includes, but is not limited to: individual health conditions, treatments, diseases, or diagnoses; health-related surgeries or procedures; use or purchase of prescribed medication; bodily functions, vital signs, symptoms, or measurements; and any information that identifies a consumer seeking health care services.

“Process” or “Processing” means any operation or set of operations performed on Consumer Health Data, including but not limited to collecting, using, storing, disclosing, analyzing, or deleting such data.

“Sale” means the exchange of Consumer Health Data for monetary or other valuable consideration. We do not sell your Consumer Health Data.

“Share” means disclosing Consumer Health Data to a third party for cross-context behavioral advertising purposes. We do not share your Consumer Health Data for advertising purposes.

03

Categories of Consumer Health Data We Collect

We may collect the following categories of Consumer Health Data when you use our Services:

•

Symptom & Health Concern Information — Symptoms, complaints, and health questions you voluntarily submit

•

Health Questionnaire Responses — Answers to assessments and intake questions within our Services

•

Health History — Background medical or wellness information you choose to provide

•

Biomarker & Lab Data — Any test results or biomarker values you voluntarily enter

•

Wellness Tracking Data — Physical and mental wellness inputs submitted through our tracking features

•

Health-Related Communications — Messages you send us through our support channels that contain health information

We collect this data solely to provide and improve our Services to you. If you’d prefer we don’t receive certain data, simply don’t submit it. Your use of our Services is always your choice.

04

Your Rights

Under applicable Consumer Health Data Privacy laws, including the Washington My Health My Data Act and Nevada SB370, you have the following rights:

✓

Right to Confirm — Ask us whether we collect, share, or sell your Consumer Health Data

✓

Right to Access — Request a copy of the Consumer Health Data we hold about you and a list of any third parties with whom it was shared

✓

Right to Correct — Ask us to fix inaccurate Consumer Health Data we hold about you

✓

Right to Withdraw Consent — Revoke previously granted permission for collection or processing of your Consumer Health Data at any time

✓

Right to Delete — Request that we delete your Consumer Health Data from our systems

✓

Right Against Discrimination — We will never discriminate against you for exercising any of your rights

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@diagnosethis.com with the subject line “Consumer Health Data Rights Request.” We will respond within 45 days.

05

Contact Us

For questions about this Policy or to submit a data rights request or appeal, contact us at:

Email: privacy@diagnosethis.com

Mail: Core DT Solutions LLC, 5030 Anchor Way, Suite 13, Christiansted, VI 00820

To appeal a data rights decision, email privacy@diagnosethis.com with the subject line “Consumer Health Data Rights Appeal.”

Review our full Privacy Policy or learn how to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information.
