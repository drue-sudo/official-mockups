01

Overview

Core DT Solutions LLC (“DiagnoseThis,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your right to control how your personal information is used. Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), and similar state laws, you have the right to direct us not to sell or share your personal information.

We do not sell your Consumer Health Data. We do not share your personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising purposes. However, we want to make sure you understand your full rights under applicable law and how you can exercise them at any time.

This page describes the categories of personal information we collect, your rights under applicable law, and exactly how to submit an opt-out request.

02

What We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal information when you use our Services:

•

Identifiers — Name, email address, username, account ID, and IP address

•

Consumer Health Information — Symptoms, health history, questionnaire responses, and wellness tracking data you voluntarily submit

•

Usage Data — Pages visited, features used, session duration, and interaction patterns within our Services

•

Communications — Messages you send us through support or contact channels

•

Device & Technical Information — Browser type, operating system, referring URLs, and device identifiers

We use this information to provide our Services, communicate with you, and improve your experience. We do not sell or share Consumer Health Data with third parties for their marketing purposes.

03

Your Rights

Under the CCPA/CPRA and applicable state privacy laws, you have the right to:

✓

Right to Know — Request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we collect, use, disclose, or sell

✓

Right to Delete — Request deletion of the personal information we have collected from you, subject to certain exceptions

✓

Right to Correct — Request correction of inaccurate personal information we maintain about you

✓

Right to Opt Out — Direct us not to sell or share your personal information with third parties

✓

Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Information — Limit our use of sensitive personal information to only what is necessary to provide the requested service

✓

Right to Non-Discrimination — We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your privacy rights

To submit a request, email privacy@diagnosethis.com. We will respond to verifiable consumer requests within 45 days. We may extend the response period by an additional 45 days with notice.

04

How to Opt Out

To submit an opt-out of sale or sharing of your personal information, follow these steps:

1

Email us at privacy@diagnosethis.com with the subject line “Do Not Sell or Share My Information”

2

Include your full name and the email address associated with your DiagnoseThis account

3

We will process your request within 15 business days and confirm via email

You may also authorize an agent to submit an opt-out request on your behalf. Authorized agents must provide written permission signed by you, or a valid power of attorney. We may contact you directly to verify the agent’s authority.

Once you opt out, we will not sell or share your personal information unless you subsequently provide explicit written consent to do so. Your opt-out will not affect the quality or availability of any Services we provide to you.

05

Contact Us

For questions about this page, to exercise your rights, or to submit an appeal of a prior decision, contact us at:

Email: privacy@diagnosethis.com

Mail: Core DT Solutions LLC, 5030 Anchor Way, Suite 13, Christiansted, VI 00820

To appeal a privacy rights decision, email privacy@diagnosethis.com with the subject line “Privacy Rights Appeal.” We will respond within 45 days.

Review our full Privacy Policy or Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy for more detail on your rights.
